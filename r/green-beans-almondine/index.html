Skillet Green Beans with Toasted Almonds

The green bean dish that replaces the casserole. Ten minutes, one pan, no canned soup, and it tastes like green beans on purpose.

15 min active 15 min total serves 8 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Green beans, trimmed
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ cup Raw almonds, sliced
  • 2 Shallot, thinly sliced
  • 3 cloves Garlic, sliced
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the beans in well-salted water for 4 minutes, then drop them into ice water. This can happen the morning of — it is the whole reason this dish is calm on a holiday.
  2. Toast the almonds in a dry skillet over medium, 3 minutes, until they smell nutty. Tip them out.
  3. Warm the oil with the shallots over medium, 4 minutes, until soft and just golden. Add garlic and pepper flakes for 45 seconds.
  4. Add the drained beans and toss over high heat 2-3 minutes until hot and blistered in places.
  5. Off heat: salt, pepper, lemon juice and the almonds. Serve immediately, while the almonds are still loud.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Blanch and shock. Bright green beans on a holiday table, among a sea of beige, do more work than any garnish.
🧒 For the kids
Pull a handful of plain blanched beans before the garlic goes in. Cold blanched green beans are a genuinely popular kid snack.
🌿 From the garden
Green beans in July freeze well after blanching — this is what to do with them in November.
🥡 Leftovers
3 days. Good cold, chopped into a salad.
