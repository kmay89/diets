The Everyday Big Salad

Enough of a salad to be dinner. The rule is five textures, and once you see it you cannot unsee it.

A
15 min active 15 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan

Ingredients

  • 1 head Romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 3 cups Arugula
  • 1 Cucumber, half-moons
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 Carrots, shaved with a peeler
  • 1 can Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 1 Avocado, sliced
  • 4 tbsp Pumpkin seeds (pepitas), toasted
  • 3 tbsp Dried cranberries
  • 2 oz Feta cheese, crumbled (optional)
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp Black pepper

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add any leftover protein

Sliced chicken, flaked salmon, or a jammy egg.

Method

  1. Dry the greens properly. Wet greens repel dressing and it pools at the bottom of the bowl — a salad spinner is the highest-value ten dollars in a kitchen.
  2. Whisk oil, lemon, Dijon and pepper in the bottom of the big bowl, then pile the sturdy things on top: carrots, cucumbers, chickpeas, cranberries.
  3. Add greens last and toss with your hands from the bottom up, twenty seconds, until every leaf is barely coated.
  4. Top with avocado, pepitas and feta. Five textures: crisp, creamy, chewy, crunchy, juicy. Check that you have all five.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Dress in the bowl you built it in and toss with your hands. Never pour dressing over the top of a finished salad.
🧒 For the kids
Give a kid a plate of the components in rows instead of a tossed bowl. Salad refusal is usually about mixing, not vegetables.
🌿 From the garden
June through October this salad is 80% backyard.
🥡 Leftovers
Dressed salad does not keep. Store components separately.
