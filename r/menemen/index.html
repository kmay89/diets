Menemen

Turkish soft-scrambled eggs cooked into peppers and tomato until it is almost a stew. Fifteen minutes, one pan, eaten with bread and no cutlery.

F
15 min active 20 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Green bell pepper, diced small
  • 5 Roma tomatoes, grated or finely chopped
  • 6 Eggs (large), lightly beaten
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • 3 oz Feta cheese, crumbled
  • ⅜ cup Flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 6 slices Sourdough bread, for serving

Method

  1. Cook the peppers in the olive oil over medium heat for 8 minutes until completely soft. Turkish cooks use long green sivri peppers; a green bell is the closest common substitute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, pepper flakes and salt and cook 6-8 minutes until the mixture thickens and the oil separates out at the edges.
  3. Turn the heat to low, pour in the eggs, and stir slowly with a wooden spoon. Menemen is meant to stay soft and loose — take it off the heat while it still looks a little wet.
  4. Crumble the feta over, scatter parsley, and bring the pan to the table with a lot of bread. There are no plates in this recipe.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Grate the tomatoes on a box grater and discard the skin. Thirty seconds, and no skin curls in the pan.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the pepper flakes out. Eggs and tomato with bread for scooping is an easy morning.
🥡 Leftovers
Eat immediately; scrambled eggs do not keep.
