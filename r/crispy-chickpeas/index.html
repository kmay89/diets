Crispy Roasted Chickpeas

The snack that replaces the chip bag. Six grams of fiber a serving and they cost about fifty cents.

5 min active 45 min total serves 6 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained, rinsed, dried very well
  • 1 ½ tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Dry the chickpeas on a towel and roll them around — genuinely dry, or they steam. Rub off any loose skins.
  2. Toss with oil only (no spices yet) and roast 35-40 minutes, shaking twice, until they rattle and one crushes with a crack.
  3. Toss with the spices and salt while hot. Spices added before roasting scorch and turn bitter.
  4. Cool uncovered. They crisp further as they cool.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Scatter them over salads, soups and grain bowls in place of croutons.
🧒 For the kids
Cinnamon and a teaspoon of maple instead of the savory spices makes a sweet version that goes in a lunchbox.
🌿 From the garden
None.
🥡 Leftovers
Best the same day. Store uncovered at room temperature — a sealed container makes them chewy overnight.
