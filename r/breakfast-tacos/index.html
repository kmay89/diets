Austin Breakfast Tacos

Soft eggs, crisp potato, salsa and a warm tortilla. Texas eats these every morning and they take fifteen minutes.

F
20 min active 25 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 Yukon gold potatoes, diced small
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • 8 Eggs (large)
  • 1 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 1 can Black beans, canned no-salt-added, drained and warmed
  • 8 Flour tortillas
  • 3 oz Monterey Jack, grated
  • ¾ cup Salsa
  • 1 Avocado, sliced
  • ⅜ cup Cilantro
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Bacon or chorizo

Cook it first and fry the potatoes in the fat. That is the Austin taqueria way.

Method

  1. Fry the diced potato in the oil over medium-high with the paprika and half the salt, undisturbed for the first 5 minutes, then turning, 12 minutes total until crisp.
  2. Beat the eggs with the rest of the salt. Cook them in the butter over low heat, stirring constantly with a spatula, 4-5 minutes. Low and slow gives you soft curds; high heat gives you rubber.
  3. Pull the eggs off the heat while they still look slightly underdone — they keep cooking in the pan.
  4. Warm the tortillas directly over a flame or in a dry skillet until they puff and blister.
  5. Build: cheese first so it melts against the hot tortilla, then eggs, potato, beans, salsa, avocado, cilantro.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Cheese against the tortilla, not on top. It melts into a layer that holds the taco together.
🧒 For the kids
Cheese and egg only, folded into a small tortilla. Salsa on the side.
🥡 Leftovers
The potato and beans keep 4 days; scramble eggs to order.
