Grilled Vegetable Platter with Charred Scallion Sauce

The vegetable dish that stops being a side. Everything hits the grill, then gets piled on a platter under a bright green sauce.

25 min active 40 min total serves 8 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 Zucchini, sliced lengthwise
  • 1 Eggplant, sliced into rounds
  • 3 Red bell pepper, quartered
  • 2 Red onion, cut into thick rings
  • 1 bunch Asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 bunch Scallions (green onions)
  • 6 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 cup Flat-leaf parsley
  • 3 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 tbsp Red wine vinegar
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 3 oz Feta cheese, crumbled (optional)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add chicken skewers or shrimp

Same grill, same sauce, cooked after the vegetables come off.

Method

  1. Heat the grill hot. Toss all the vegetables with 3 tbsp oil and half the salt.
  2. Grill the whole bunch of scallions first, 3 minutes, until charred and floppy. Set aside — they are the sauce.
  3. Grill everything else in batches: peppers and onions 4-5 minutes a side, zucchini and eggplant 3-4, asparagus 3. Do not crowd the grate and do not fuss with them.
  4. Blend the charred scallions with garlic, parsley, lemon juice, vinegar, pepper flakes, the remaining 3 tbsp oil and the rest of the salt until coarse and vividly green.
  5. Pile the vegetables on a big platter — warm or room temperature, not hot — and spoon the sauce over. Feta if you want it.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Room temperature, not hot. Grilled vegetables taste more of themselves once they have settled for twenty minutes, which also means you are not cooking while guests arrive.
🧒 For the kids
Grilled peppers and zucchini are sweet enough that most kids eat them plain. Keep a corner of the platter sauce-free.
🌿 From the garden
This is the August garden on a platter.
🥡 Leftovers
4 days. Chop into a grain bowl, or layer into a sandwich with the leftover sauce.
