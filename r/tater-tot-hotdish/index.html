Tater Tot Hotdish

The Upper Midwest's most sincere casserole, made without a can of condensed soup — a real mushroom gravy takes ten minutes and tastes like ten times more.

F
25 min active 65 min total serves 6 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Brown lentils (dry), cooked
  • 4 cups Cremini mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 3 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp All-purpose flour
  • 2 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • ¾ cup 1% milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 1 ½ cups Frozen peas
  • 1 cup Corn kernels
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 5 cups Frozen potato tots
  • 3 oz Sharp cheddar, grated

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Ground beef

A pound of 90% lean, browned and drained, in place of the lentils.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Brown the mushrooms and onion in the butter over high heat for 10 minutes until the pan is dry and everything is well colored.
  2. Stir in the flour and cook 2 minutes. Add the broth and milk gradually, whisking, and simmer 4 minutes until it thickens into a gravy. This is what the can of soup was standing in for.
  3. Stir in the mustard, thyme, lentils, peas, corn, salt and pepper. Spread in the baking dish.
  4. Line the tots up on top in tidy rows — this matters to people from Minnesota — and bake 35 minutes.
  5. Scatter the cheese over and bake 8 minutes more until the tots are crisp and the cheese has melted. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Do not cover the dish. The whole appeal is a crisp tot roof, and steam is its enemy.
🧒 For the kids
This is the recipe children request. Serve it with something raw and green on the side to balance the plate.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days. Reheat in the oven, not the microwave, or the tots go soft.
