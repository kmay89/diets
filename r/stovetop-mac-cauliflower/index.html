Stovetop Mac and Cheese with Hidden Cauliflower

Cauliflower simmered soft and blended into the sauce, where it disappears completely and makes the whole thing creamier. No roux, no baking dish, one pot.

F
20 min active 25 min total serves 5 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 10 oz Whole wheat pasta (dry)
  • 3 cups Cauliflower, cut small
  • 1 cup 1% milk
  • 1 ¼ cups Sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp Black pepper
  • ⅜ cup Panko breadcrumbs (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil (optional)

Method

  1. Put the cauliflower in a large pot with the milk and a pinch of the salt. Cover and cook over medium for 10 minutes, until a fork goes through with no resistance.
  2. Blend the cauliflower and milk smooth — an immersion blender in the pot is easiest. This is the grown-up step.
  3. Meanwhile cook the pasta in salted water, one minute short of the package time, and drain it.
  4. Put the cauliflower purée back over low heat. Add the cheddar a handful at a time, stirring until each one disappears before the next goes in. Keep it off a boil or the sauce goes grainy.
  5. Stir in the mustard, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Taste it — this is where it either becomes mac and cheese or stays cauliflower sauce, and the answer is almost always more salt.
  6. Fold the pasta in. If you want a crunchy top, toast the panko in the olive oil in a small pan until golden and scatter it over the bowls.
  7. Serve straight away, while it is at its creamiest.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The toasted panko. Thirty seconds of work, and it turns a soft bowl into something with a texture in it.
🧒 For the kids
Do not hide the cauliflower — show them. A vegetable that made the sauce creamier is a much better story than a vegetable that was smuggled in, and it survives being discovered.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 3 days. Reheat over low heat with a splash of milk, stirring, rather than in the microwave on high.
