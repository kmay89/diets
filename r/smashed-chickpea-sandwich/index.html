Smashed Chickpea Salad Sandwiches

Tastes like the tuna salad you grew up on, built on chickpeas and Greek yogurt instead of mayo. Fifteen minutes and it packs for lunch all week.

B
15 min active 15 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • ⅜ cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 stalks Celery, finely diced
  • ¼ Red onion, minced
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 tbsp Capers, rinsed and chopped (optional)
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • 8 slices Whole grain bread
  • 4 cups Romaine lettuce, leaves
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, sliced

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add canned tuna

Half the chickpea mix plus a drained can of tuna is a natural for the omnivores.

Method

  1. Smash the chickpeas with a masher or fork until about two thirds are broken down and a third stay whole. Texture is everything here.
  2. Fold in yogurt, Dijon, celery, red onion, dill, lemon juice, capers, salt and pepper.
  3. Taste and adjust — it usually wants more lemon and more pepper than you expect.
  4. Build the sandwiches with lettuce against the bread on both sides so it does not go soggy in a lunchbox.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Rinsed chopped capers. A tablespoon adds the briny pop that makes people ask what is in it.
🧒 For the kids
Serve it as a scoop with crackers and cut vegetables instead of a sandwich. Same food, more likely eaten.
🌿 From the garden
Dill, and lettuce in spring and fall.
🥡 Leftovers
The salad keeps 4 days. Do not build the sandwich until you are eating it.
