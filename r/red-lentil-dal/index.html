25-Minute Red Lentil Dal

Red lentils collapse into something creamy with no cream at all. Finished with a sizzling spice oil poured over at the table.

A
12 min active 30 min total serves 5 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Canola oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp Ground turmeric
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 1 ½ cups Red lentils (dry), rinsed until the water runs clear
  • 4 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 can Diced tomatoes, no salt added
  • 1 cup Light coconut milk
  • 4 cups Baby spinach
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 Lime
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • ½ cup Cilantro, chopped
  • 1 ½ cups Jasmine rice (dry), dry

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add roasted chicken thighs

Rub with the same spice mix and roast while the dal simmers.

Method

  1. Start the rice. Then heat canola oil in a saucepan over medium, cook the onion 6 minutes, add garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
  2. Add turmeric, cumin and coriander; stir 30 seconds until they smell toasty.
  3. Add lentils, broth, tomatoes and salt. Simmer uncovered 18-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils have completely broken down.
  4. Stir in coconut milk and spinach; cook 2 minutes until the spinach wilts. Squeeze in lime juice.
  5. The tarka: heat olive oil in a small skillet until shimmering, add pepper flakes and garam masala, count to ten, and pour it sizzling over the pot. Stir once.
  6. Serve over jasmine rice with cilantro.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The sizzling spice oil poured at the table is theater, and it takes 60 seconds. Do it in front of everyone.
🧒 For the kids
Hold the tarka off the kids' bowls — it carries all the heat. Dal over rice with a spoon of yogurt is about as kid-safe as dinner gets.
🌿 From the garden
Nothing to grow here except cilantro, which bolts in July heat — sow a new short row every three weeks.
🥡 Leftovers
Thickens overnight into something closer to a spread. Loosen with water. Freezes 3 months.
