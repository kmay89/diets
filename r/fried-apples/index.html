Skillet Fried Apples

Apples cooked in butter with cinnamon until they slump and caramelize. A side dish in Appalachia, a dessert everywhere else, and fifteen minutes either way.

10 min active 20 min total serves 6 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 6 Apples, cored and cut into thick wedges
  • 3 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp Ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp Ground nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ Lemon

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a wide skillet over medium-high. Add the apples in one layer, cut side down, and leave them 4 minutes to brown.
  2. Turn, add the brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt, and cook 6-8 minutes more, turning now and then, until the apples are tender and the syrup has darkened and thickened.
  3. A firm apple is essential — Granny Smith, Northern Spy, Jonathan. A soft one turns to sauce, which is a different dish.
  4. Finish with a squeeze of lemon so it does not read as pure sugar. Serve alongside eggs, over yogurt, or with ice cream.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Do not stir for the first four minutes. Browned apple faces are the difference between fried apples and stewed apples.
🧒 For the kids
Serve over plain yogurt for breakfast and it is a genuinely good one.
🌿 From the garden
Northeast Ohio is apple country from September through November. A half bushel of seconds from an orchard costs almost nothing and makes this all fall.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and reheats in a pan in two minutes.
