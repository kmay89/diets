Za'atar Sheet-Pan Chickpeas and Vegetables with Tahini

One pan, twenty-five minutes in the oven, and a lemon-tahini sauce that makes it feel composed rather than thrown together.

A
15 min active 45 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained, rinsed, patted very dry
  • 1 head Cauliflower, florets
  • 4 Carrots, cut on the bias
  • 1 Red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Za'atar
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • ⅜ cup Tahini
  • 3 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 clove Garlic, grated
  • ½ cup Flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 cup Whole wheat couscous (dry), dry
  • 3 tbsp Pumpkin seeds (pepitas)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add za'atar chicken thighs

Third pan, same seasoning, same oven, 22 minutes.

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Dry the chickpeas thoroughly on a towel — wet chickpeas steam instead of crisping.
  2. Toss cauliflower, carrots and onion with 2 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp za'atar, cumin and salt on one pan. Toss chickpeas with the remaining oil and za'atar on the second.
  3. Roast both 25-30 minutes, stirring once. The chickpeas should rattle and the cauliflower should be brown at the tips.
  4. Tahini sauce: whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic and a pinch of salt. It will seize into a paste — keep whisking in cold water a tablespoon at a time until it turns pale and pourable.
  5. Make the couscous: 1 cup couscous, 1.25 cups boiling water, covered, 5 minutes, fluff.
  6. Pile everything over couscous, pour tahini over generously, and finish with parsley and pepitas.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Pour the tahini in a wide zigzag over the whole platter rather than serving it on the side. It reads as a finished dish.
🧒 For the kids
The crispy chickpeas are the sell. Give a kid a bowl of just those plus plain couscous and tahini for dipping.
🌿 From the garden
Nothing here needs a garden, which is exactly why this is the February recipe.
🥡 Leftovers
Chickpeas soften; roast a fresh half batch if you care, or accept them soft in a wrap with the tahini.
