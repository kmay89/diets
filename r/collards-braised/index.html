Braised Collard Greens

An hour on low, a splash of vinegar, and greens that taste like they had a ham hock in them. Save the pot liquor — it is the best part.

15 min active 70 min total serves 6 vegetarianvegan

Ingredients

  • 3 bunchs Collard greens, stemmed, sliced into ribbons
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, sliced
  • 5 cloves Garlic, sliced
  • 1 ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 3 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Molasses
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Cook the onion in the oil over medium for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, paprika and pepper flakes for 1 minute.
  2. Add the collards a few handfuls at a time, letting each batch wilt before the next goes in.
  3. Pour in the broth, 2 tablespoons of the vinegar, the molasses and the salt. Cover and simmer very gently for 45-60 minutes. Collards are tough and they want the time — this is not a quick sauté vegetable.
  4. Finish with the last tablespoon of vinegar off the heat. Serve with some of the pot liquor spooned over, and cornbread for dipping.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Molasses and vinegar together. Sweet and sour is what keeps a pot of long-cooked greens from being flat.
🧒 For the kids
Cut a smaller portion with a lot of the sweet pot liquor and some cornbread crumbled in. That is how most Southern children first meet collards.
🌿 From the garden
Collards are the easiest winter crop in zone 6a. They survive hard frost and get sweeter for it — pick from October into January.
🥡 Leftovers
Improves for three days. Keeps 5.
