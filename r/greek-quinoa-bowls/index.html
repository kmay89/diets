Greek Quinoa Bowls with Whipped Feta

Everything cold and crunchy over warm quinoa, with a whipped feta that takes two minutes and tastes like a restaurant meze plate.

B
20 min active 30 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups Quinoa (dry), dry, rinsed
  • 1 can Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 1 Cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ Red onion, thinly sliced
  • ⅜ cup Kalamata olives, halved
  • 4 oz Feta cheese
  • ½ cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 head Romaine lettuce, chopped
  • ½ tsp Black pepper

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add oregano-lemon chicken

Marinate in the morning, grill or roast in 20 minutes at dinner.

Method

  1. Cook the quinoa in 2.5 cups water, 15 minutes, then rest covered 5 minutes off heat and fluff. Toss warm with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp lemon juice so it absorbs the dressing.
  2. Whipped feta: blend the feta with the yogurt and 1 tbsp lemon juice for 60 seconds until it is smooth and spreadable. Loosen with a spoon of water if needed.
  3. Whisk the remaining lemon juice, 3 tbsp olive oil, oregano and black pepper into a dressing. No salt needed — the feta and olives bring plenty.
  4. Toss cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, olives and chickpeas with half the dressing.
  5. Build: a swipe of whipped feta on the bottom of each bowl, warm quinoa, romaine, the vegetable mix, the rest of the dressing, and dill over everything.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The whipped feta swiped across the bottom of the bowl instead of crumbled on top. It costs nothing and looks like a $19 lunch.
🧒 For the kids
Serve it as a plate, not a bowl — quinoa in one spot, cucumbers and tomatoes in another, feta dip with pita on the side. Kids who won't eat a mixed bowl will eat every component.
🌿 From the garden
Cucumbers, cherry tomatoes and dill all peak together in late July, which is exactly when you want this.
🥡 Leftovers
Best meal-prep lunch in the collection. Keep dressing separate; assembled bowls hold 4 days.
