Butternut and Chickpea Curry

Sweet squash, creamy chickpeas, and a coconut broth that tastes like it took an hour. It takes thirty-five minutes and one pot.

B
15 min active 40 min total serves 5 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Canola oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
  • 5 cups Butternut squash, peeled, 1-inch cubes
  • 1 can Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can Light coconut milk
  • 1 cup Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 4 cups Baby spinach
  • 1 Lime
  • ½ cup Cilantro
  • 3 tbsp Pumpkin seeds (pepitas), toasted
  • 1 ½ cups Jasmine rice (dry), dry

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add poached chicken or seared shrimp

Poach chicken pieces directly in a ladle of the curry in a small pot for 10 minutes so it tastes like the same dish.

Method

  1. Cook the onion in oil over medium 6 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, 1 minute.
  2. Add the curry paste and fry it in the oil 90 seconds until it darkens and the kitchen smells like a restaurant. This is the step people skip and it is the whole flavor.
  3. Add squash, chickpeas, coconut milk and broth. Simmer covered 20 minutes until the squash yields to a knife but still holds its shape.
  4. Stir in spinach until wilted, 2 minutes. Finish with lime juice — taste, and add more; the acid is what makes coconut curry taste finished rather than heavy.
  5. Serve over jasmine rice with cilantro and toasted pepitas.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Toasted pepitas on top for crunch and a lime wedge on every plate.
🧒 For the kids
Curry paste brands vary wildly in heat. Start with 2 tbsp, taste after step 3, and add more for the adults at the end.
🌿 From the garden
Butternut stores in a cool basement until February — one summer's harvest covers a winter of these.
🥡 Leftovers
Freezes beautifully for 3 months. The squash gets softer; nobody minds.
