Grilled Romaine Caesar

Halved romaine hearts on a hot grate for ninety seconds — warm and smoky at the base, cold and crisp at the tips. The dressing is anchovy-free and still tastes like Caesar.

15 min active 20 min total serves 4 vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 heads Romaine lettuce, halved lengthwise, root left on
  • 5 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves Garlic, grated
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp Capers, rinsed and minced
  • 1 Lemon
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated, plus shavings
  • 3 tbsp Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 3 slices Sourdough bread, torn into croutons

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Anchovy, the original

Two oil-packed anchovy fillets mashed into the dressing. It is the traditional version and it is better.

Method

  1. Whisk 3 tablespoons of the oil with the garlic, mustard, minced capers, lemon juice, yogurt, grated parmesan and pepper. The capers do what anchovy does — briny, savory, sharp — and this dressing needs no salt beyond them.
  2. Toss the torn bread with 1 tablespoon oil and grill or toast until crisp at the edges and chewy in the middle.
  3. Brush the cut faces of the romaine with the last tablespoon of oil.
  4. Grill cut side down over high heat for 90 seconds. No longer — you are marking it, not cooking it. The center should still be cold.
  5. Lay the halves cut side up, spoon the dressing into the leaves, add croutons and shave more parmesan over. Serve immediately with a knife and fork.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Leave the root end on. It holds the half together on the grate and comes off in one cut at the table.
🧒 For the kids
Warm lettuce is a hard sell. Keep a few raw leaves aside with dressing for dipping and croutons on the side.
🥡 Leftovers
Eat it hot. The dressing keeps 5 days and is excellent on anything.
