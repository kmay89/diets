Thai Green Curry

Cracking the coconut cream and frying the paste in it is the step everyone skips and the one that matters. Twenty-five minutes for something that tastes like it took hours.

D
20 min active 30 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan

Ingredients

  • 1 can Full-fat coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 1 can Light coconut milk
  • 1 block Extra-firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 Eggplant, cut into chunks
  • 1 Red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 cups Green beans, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 Lime
  • 1 cup Fresh basil, leaves, Thai basil if you can find it
  • 1 ½ cups Jasmine rice (dry)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Chicken and fish sauce

Sliced thigh added with the eggplant, and fish sauce in place of the soy — that is the standard Thai seasoning and it is noticeably better.

Method

  1. Start the rice. Open the can of full-fat coconut milk without shaking it and spoon the thick cream off the top into a wide pot over medium heat.
  2. Cook that cream 3-4 minutes until it separates and you can see clear oil pooling at the edges — this is called cracking the cream. Add the curry paste and fry it in that oil 2 minutes until it darkens and the kitchen smells like a Thai restaurant.
  3. Add the rest of both cans and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add the eggplant first and simmer 6 minutes, then the green beans, pepper and tofu for 5 more. The eggplant should be silky, the beans still bright.
  5. Season with the sugar, soy sauce and a good squeeze of lime. Taste and keep adjusting — Thai food is balanced at the end, not the beginning.
  6. Off the heat, stir in the basil leaves whole. Serve over jasmine rice with more lime.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Basil goes in off the heat, whole. Cooked basil in a curry tastes like hay.
🧒 For the kids
Green curry paste is the hottest of the three colors. Start with a tablespoon for a family with children and put sriracha on the table for the adults.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and is better the next day. The vegetables soften, so it becomes a different and still good dish.
