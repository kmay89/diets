Air Fryer Churros

A real choux paste piped, air fried and rolled in cinnamon sugar, with a proper chocolate sauce for dipping. Thirty minutes, no vat of oil.

25 min active 35 min total serves 6 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 cup All-purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 2 Eggs (large)
  • ½ cup Granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • 2 tsp Ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 4 oz Dark chocolate (70%)
  • ½ cup 1% milk
  • 1 tbsp Canola oil

Method

  1. Bring 1 cup water, the butter, salt and 1 tablespoon of the sugar to a boil. Dump in the flour all at once and stir hard over the heat for 2 minutes until it forms a smooth ball that leaves a film on the pan bottom.
  2. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time and the vanilla. It will look broken after each egg and come back together — keep beating.
  3. Pipe 5-inch lengths through a large star tip straight into the air fryer basket, lined with perforated paper. The ridges from the star tip are structural: they give the churro its crisp edges.
  4. Mist with oil and air fry at 375°F for 10-12 minutes until golden and firm.
  5. Toss the hot churros in the remaining sugar mixed with the cinnamon.
  6. Sauce: warm the milk and pour it over the chopped chocolate, then stir until glossy. Spanish chocolate for churros is thick enough to coat — it is a dipping sauce, not a drink.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Sugar them while they are hot. Cold churros will not hold the coating.
🧒 For the kids
Piping is the fun part. Let them do it and accept the shapes.
🥡 Leftovers
Eat immediately — this is genuinely a to-order dessert.
