Banana Pudding

A real stovetop custard rather than a box of instant, layered with wafers and bananas and left overnight to go soft. Southern potluck royalty.

25 min active 260 min total serves 10 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 cups 1% milk
  • ⅝ cup Granulated sugar
  • 5 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 4 Eggs (large), yolks only
  • 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • 5 Bananas, sliced
  • 30 Ladyfingers (savoiardi), or vanilla wafers
  • 1 cup Heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp Powdered sugar

Method

  1. Whisk the sugar, cornstarch and salt in a saucepan, then whisk in the milk gradually so there are no lumps. Cook over medium, whisking constantly, until it thickens and just bubbles, about 8 minutes.
  2. Temper the yolks: whisk a cup of the hot mixture into the beaten yolks, then pour it all back into the pan. Cook 2 minutes more, whisking hard. Skipping the tempering gives you sweet scrambled eggs.
  3. Off the heat, whisk in the butter and vanilla. Strain it through a sieve — a thirty-second insurance policy against any lumps.
  4. Layer in the dish: custard, wafers, bananas, repeating and finishing with custard. Press wrap onto the surface and chill 4 hours or overnight.
  5. Before serving, whip the cream with the powdered sugar to soft peaks and spread it over. The wafers should have gone completely soft — that is the whole point and it takes the full four hours.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Strain the custard. Every professional pastry kitchen does it and it takes half a minute.
🧒 For the kids
Assemble it in individual jars and let each child layer their own.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 2 days; the bananas darken. Make it the day before, not two.
