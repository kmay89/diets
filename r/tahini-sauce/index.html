Everything Tahini Sauce

The sauce that makes roasted vegetables into dinner. Four ingredients, two minutes, keeps a week.

5 min active 5 min total serves 8 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Tahini
  • 4 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 clove Garlic, grated
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin (optional)
  • 1 tsp Pure maple syrup (optional)

Method

  1. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic and salt. It will seize into a stiff paste and look ruined. It is not.
  2. Whisk in cold water a tablespoon at a time — usually 4 to 6 total — and it will suddenly turn pale, smooth and pourable.
  3. Thin further for drizzling, leave thicker for dipping.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Grate the garlic on a microplane rather than mincing it so there are no raw chunks.
🧒 For the kids
Add the teaspoon of maple. It takes the edge off the sesame bitterness that kids notice and adults don't.
🌿 From the garden
Herbs blended in make a green tahini that is even better.
🥡 Leftovers
1 week refrigerated. It thickens; whisk in water.
