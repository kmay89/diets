Soft Scrambled Eggs with Wilted Greens

Low and slow, off the heat before you think, with greens wilted into the pan first. Ten minutes and it eats like a hotel breakfast.

F
12 min active 12 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 6 Eggs (large)
  • 4 Egg whites
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 cups Baby spinach
  • 2 Scallions (green onions), sliced
  • 1 clove Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Parmesan cheese, grated
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 4 slices Whole grain bread, toasted
  • 1 Avocado, sliced (optional)

Method

  1. Beat the eggs and whites with salt until the color is completely uniform — streaky eggs cook unevenly.
  2. Wilt the spinach with the garlic in the oil over medium, 2 minutes, until the water has cooked off. Scrape it onto the toast.
  3. Wipe the pan, set it over low heat — genuinely low — and add the eggs. Stir constantly with a rubber spatula, pushing curds from the edge to the center.
  4. After 3-4 minutes they will start to set into small soft curds. Pull the pan off the heat while they still look slightly underdone; carryover finishes them.
  5. Fold in parmesan and scallions. Pile onto the greens and toast with black pepper and avocado.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Low heat and constant motion. That is the entire difference between diner eggs and restaurant eggs.
🧒 For the kids
Cook a kid's portion a minute longer and skip the greens under theirs — put the spinach on the side where they can ignore it, which is how they eventually try it.
🌿 From the garden
Spring spinach and chives.
🥡 Leftovers
None. Scrambled eggs do not keep and everybody knows it.
