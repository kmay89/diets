Appalachian Soup Beans

Pinto beans cooked down until the broth is thick and brown, eaten with cornbread and raw onion. A dollar a bowl, and it built a region.

A
15 min active 100 min total serves 6 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 cans Pinto beans, canned no-salt-added, two drained, one with liquid
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, smashed
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 4 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • ½ Red onion, diced, for the table
  • 2 tsp Louisiana-style hot sauce

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

A ham hock

One smoked hock in the pot from the beginning, pulled at the end and the meat shredded back in.

Method

  1. Cook the onion in the oil over medium for 8 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika for 1.
  2. Add the beans, bay leaves and broth. Simmer, partly covered, 60-75 minutes, until the liquid has gone thick and brown. If you start from a pound of dried pintos soaked overnight, give it two hours and salt only at the end.
  3. Mash a cup of the beans against the side of the pot. The broth — soup beans are named for it — should coat a spoon.
  4. Add the salt, pepper and vinegar. Taste; it will want more pepper than you expect.
  5. Serve in bowls with a wedge of skillet cornbread, raw onion on top, and hot sauce. Crumble the cornbread in — that is the point of the bowl.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The mash and the vinegar. Two moves, thirty seconds, and it goes from beans in water to soup beans.
🧒 For the kids
Beans and cornbread. Serve the raw onion on the side.
🥡 Leftovers
Better every day for four. Freezes 3 months.
