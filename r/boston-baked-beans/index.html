Boston Baked Beans

Molasses, mustard and onion, and four hours in a low oven. It is why the city is called Beantown, and it is almost entirely unattended time.

15 min active 260 min total serves 8 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 cans Cannellini beans, canned no-salt-added, drained, or navy beans
  • ⅜ cup Molasses
  • 3 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp Yellow mustard
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 2 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Salt pork or bacon

A quarter pound of salt pork buried in the middle of the pot is the traditional version.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Stir everything together in a heavy pot with a lid — the beans should be just covered with liquid.
  2. Cover and bake 3 hours, checking every hour and adding hot water if they look dry.
  3. Uncover and bake 1 hour more. The top darkens and the liquid reduces to a thick, glossy syrup. That last hour is where the color comes from.
  4. Taste for salt and vinegar. They should be sweet, sour, smoky and dark all at once.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Molasses, not brown sugar alone. Blackstrap is bitter here; plain unsulphured molasses is what makes this taste like Boston.
🧒 For the kids
Sweet baked beans are an easy sell. Serve with brown bread or cornbread.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps a week and gets better. Freezes 3 months.
