Greek Chickpea Jar Salads

Four lunches built in ten minutes on Sunday. Dressing on the bottom, greens on top, and nothing is soggy on Thursday.

B
15 min active 15 min total serves 4 vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 can Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 1 Cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ Red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup Kalamata olives
  • 3 oz Feta cheese, crumbled
  • 4 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 tbsp Red wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 6 cups Romaine lettuce, chopped
  • ¾ cup Quinoa (dry), dry, cooked (optional)

Method

  1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, oregano and Dijon. Divide among four jars — dressing always goes in first.
  2. Layer in this order and do not improvise it: chickpeas and red onion (they marinate), then cucumber and tomatoes, then quinoa, then feta and olives, then romaine packed on top.
  3. Seal and refrigerate up to 4 days. Shake into a bowl when you eat.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Marinating the onion and chickpeas in the dressing for days is not a compromise — it is genuinely better than dressing a salad at the last second.
🧒 For the kids
Build a kid's version in a divided container instead: chickpeas, cucumbers, cheese, crackers. A jar you shake is an adult format.
🌿 From the garden
Cucumbers and tomatoes at their July peak.
🥡 Leftovers
That is the entire point. 4 days.
