Pressed Caprese Sandwich

Tomato, mozzarella, basil and good oil, pressed for an hour so the bread drinks the juices. Built for a cooler and better after two hours than at zero.

F
15 min active 75 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 Baguette, split lengthwise
  • 4 Roma tomatoes, sliced and salted
  • 8 oz Part-skim mozzarella, sliced
  • 1 cup Fresh basil
  • 4 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Balsamic vinegar
  • 1 clove Garlic, halved
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp Black pepper
  • 2 cups Arugula
  • ½ cup Roasted red peppers (jarred), drained (optional)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add salami or prosciutto

A few thin slices against the top half, so the fat softens into the crumb while it presses.

Method

  1. Salt the tomato slices and lay them on a towel for 10 minutes. Blot them. Unsalted, undrained tomato is what destroys a packed sandwich.
  2. Rub the cut faces of the bread with the halved garlic clove, then drizzle with half the oil and all the vinegar.
  3. Layer mozzarella first against the bottom bread, then tomato, basil, arugula, roasted peppers, pepper and the rest of the oil.
  4. Close it, wrap it tightly in parchment or foil, and put a heavy pan on top for at least an hour — up to four in the fridge.
  5. Cut into four through the wrapping. The bread should be compressed and stained pink at the edges.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The press. An hour under a cast-iron pan turns four separate ingredients into one thing.
🧒 For the kids
Cheese-and-tomato bread that has been squashed on purpose. Skip the arugula and balsamic on theirs.
🥡 Leftovers
Best 1-4 hours after pressing. Keeps overnight but the bread softens.
