Oregon Mushroom and Hazelnut Farro

Wild mushrooms, toasted hazelnuts, farro and a little cream — the Willamette Valley in a bowl. Thirty-five minutes, and it eats like a restaurant plate.

C
25 min active 40 min total serves 4 vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ cups Farro (dry)
  • 4 cups Cremini mushrooms, torn
  • 2 cups Shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cups Oyster mushrooms, torn
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 3 Shallot, sliced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 5 sprigs Fresh thyme
  • ½ cup Dry white wine
  • 1 cup Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp Heavy cream
  • ½ cup Hazelnuts, toasted, skinned, chopped
  • 4 cups Lacinato kale, shredded
  • 1 Lemon
  • ⅜ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Seared salmon on top

A fillet per person, roasted at 275°F for 20 minutes, laid over the farro.

Method

  1. Simmer the farro in well-salted water 25 minutes until chewy, and drain.
  2. Roast the hazelnuts at 350°F for 12 minutes, then rub them in a towel while warm — most of the bitter skin comes away.
  3. Brown the mushrooms in the oil over high heat in two batches, leaving each batch still for the first 3 minutes. Wet, crowded mushrooms are the single most common mistake in home cooking.
  4. Add the butter, shallots, garlic and thyme and cook 4 minutes. Pour in the wine and let it reduce almost away.
  5. Add the broth and the farro and simmer 4 minutes. Stir in the kale to wilt, then the cream, lemon juice, parmesan and salt.
  6. Serve with the hazelnuts scattered over at the last second so they stay loud.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Three tablespoons of cream for four people. Enough to bind and gloss, not enough to make it a cream sauce.
🧒 For the kids
Blend a cup of it briefly to soften the texture, and hold the thyme.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and is very good cold as a grain salad with extra lemon.
