House Lemon-Dijon Vinaigrette

One jar, ninety seconds, and you never buy bottled dressing again. A quarter of the sodium and none of the additives.

3 min active 3 min total serves 12 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Red wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Pure maple syrup
  • 1 clove Garlic, grated
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper

Method

  1. Put everything in a jar, seal it, and shake hard for 30 seconds. The Dijon is the emulsifier — without it the dressing separates in a minute.
  2. Taste on a leaf of lettuce, not off a spoon. Dressing always tastes too sharp alone.
  3. Keeps two weeks refrigerated. It solidifies cold — that is the olive oil, and it is a sign of good oil. Let it sit out 10 minutes and shake again.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Three parts oil to one part acid is the classic ratio; this runs closer to 2:1 because bright dressing makes vegetables taste better and lets you use less salt everywhere else.
🧒 For the kids
An extra teaspoon of maple syrup or honey makes it a dressing kids will actually put on salad.
🌿 From the garden
A tablespoon of chopped herbs — chives, dill, basil — turns it into a different dressing every week.
🥡 Leftovers
2 weeks refrigerated.
