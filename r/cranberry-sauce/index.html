Real Cranberry Sauce with Orange

Twelve minutes, four ingredients, and half the sugar of the canned version. The one dish where homemade is not just better, it is faster.

5 min active 15 min total serves 12 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 bag Fresh cranberries
  • ½ cup Granulated sugar
  • 1 Orange, zest and juice
  • 2 tbsp Pure maple syrup
  • ¼ tsp Ground cinnamon (optional)
  • ⅛ tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Put everything except the zest in a saucepan with 2 tbsp water. Bring to a simmer over medium.
  2. Cook 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally. The berries will pop audibly — that is the sound of it working.
  3. Pull it while about a quarter of the berries are still whole. It thickens a lot as it cools, so it should look slightly loose in the pan.
  4. Stir in the orange zest off the heat. Cool completely before serving.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Zest at the end, never at the start. Simmering drives off the aromatic oils that make it taste like oranges.
🧒 For the kids
Kids who claim to hate cranberry sauce usually mean the ridged cylinder. Let them taste this one warm off the spoon.
🌿 From the garden
Nothing — but buy an extra bag of cranberries in November and freeze it. They cook from frozen and keep a year.
🥡 Leftovers
2 weeks refrigerated. Swirl it into yogurt, or spread it on a sandwich with sharp cheddar.
