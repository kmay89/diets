Okonomiyaki

Osaka's cabbage pancake — half a head of cabbage per pan, crisp outside, custardy inside, and built to take whatever is in the fridge. Kids love making it.

C
25 min active 35 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 6 cups Green cabbage, shredded fine
  • 1 cup All-purpose flour
  • 4 Eggs (large)
  • ½ cup Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 5 Scallions (green onions), sliced
  • 1 Carrots, grated
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp Ketchup
  • 1 tsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tsp Pure maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar (unseasoned)
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds
  • 1 sheet Nori sheets, crumbled (optional)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Bacon on top, Osaka style

Lay two thin strips on the raw side before the flip. They crisp into the pancake as the second side cooks.

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, eggs and broth into a thick batter. Fold in the cabbage, carrot and most of the scallions — it will look like far too much cabbage and not enough batter. That is correct; the batter is glue, not the body.
  2. Make the okonomi sauce: stir the ketchup, maple syrup, soy sauce and rice vinegar together. It should taste sweet, tangy and savory all at once. (The bottled version is built on Worcestershire, which contains anchovy — this one does not.)
  3. Heat a tablespoon of oil in the skillet over medium. Add half the mixture and press it into a 1-inch-thick round. Cover and cook 6 minutes — covering steams the cabbage through while the bottom browns.
  4. Slide it onto a plate, invert the skillet over it, and flip. Cook uncovered 5 more minutes until the second side is deep brown and the center is set.
  5. Repeat with the rest. Cut into wedges, then zigzag the sauce over, drizzle the mayonnaise, and finish with sesame seeds, the remaining scallions and crumbled nori.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Cover the pan for the first side. That single step is what gets a fat pancake cooked through without a burnt bottom.
🧒 For the kids
Okonomi means 'as you like it'. Set the bowls out and let each child build their own small pancake — it is one of the few dinners where the cooking is the entertainment.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 2 days and reheats crisply in a dry skillet.
