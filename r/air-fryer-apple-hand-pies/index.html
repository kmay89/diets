Air Fryer Apple Hand Pies

Puff pastry, spiced apple, twelve minutes in the air fryer. Warm hand pies on a weeknight without heating the oven or frying anything.

20 min active 40 min total serves 8 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 Apples, peeled and diced small
  • 3 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 1 ½ tsp Ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp Ground nutmeg
  • 2 tsp Cornstarch
  • ½ Lemon
  • ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 sheet Puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 Eggs (large), beaten
  • 1 tbsp Granulated sugar

Method

  1. Cook the diced apple with the butter, brown sugar, spices, lemon and salt over medium for 6 minutes until softened. Stir in the cornstarch slurried with a teaspoon of water and cook 1 minute more.
  2. Cool the filling completely. Warm filling melts pastry and you get leaks.
  3. Cut the pastry into 8 rectangles. Put a heaped tablespoon of filling on half of each, fold over, and seal the edges with a fork pressed hard.
  4. Cut two small vents in each top, brush with egg, sprinkle with granulated sugar.
  5. Air fry at 350°F for 11-13 minutes until deep golden and puffed. Lower and slower than most air fryer recipes — pastry needs time for its layers to separate.
  6. Cool 10 minutes. The filling is molten and it will burn a mouth.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Vents in the top. Without them the steam blows a hole in the side and the filling ends up in the basket.
🧒 For the kids
Sealing the edges with a fork is a good job for small hands, and they are more careful about it than adults.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 2 days. Re-crisp for 3 minutes at 350°F.
