Smashed Chickpea Salad Sandwich

Ten minutes, no cooking, and it holds up in a lunchbox better than anything with meat in it. Celery and pickle do the work here.

C
10 min active 10 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 stalks Celery, diced small
  • ¼ Red onion, minced
  • 3 tbsp Dill pickles, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • ½ Lemon
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 8 slices Whole grain bread
  • 4 leaf Butter lettuce
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, sliced

Method

  1. Smash the chickpeas with a fork or a potato masher until about two-thirds are broken and a third are still whole. That mix of textures is what makes it read as a salad rather than a paste.
  2. Fold in everything else except the bread and the lettuce and tomato.
  3. Taste for salt, lemon and pepper. It should be sharper than seems right in the bowl — bread mutes everything.
  4. Build on toasted bread with lettuce laid against both slices as a moisture barrier, then tomato, then the filling.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Lettuce against the bread, not on top of the filling. It keeps a packed sandwich edible four hours later.
🧒 For the kids
Serve it as a dip with crackers and cucumber rounds instead of a sandwich. Same lunchbox, less negotiation.
🥡 Leftovers
The filling keeps 4 days. Do not build sandwiches ahead.
