Mujaddara

Lentils, rice and a mountain of deeply caramelized onions. It costs almost nothing, it feeds a crowd, and it is one of the oldest recipes in the collection — a version appears in a 13th-century Baghdadi cookbook.

B
30 min active 60 min total serves 6 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ cups Brown lentils (dry), rinsed
  • 1 cup Basmati rice, rinsed and soaked 20 minutes
  • 4 Yellow onion, sliced thin
  • 5 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • ½ tsp Ground cinnamon
  • 1 ¼ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt (optional)
  • 1 Lemon
  • 1 clove Garlic, grated
  • ½ cup Flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp Ground sumac

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add lamb or beef

Not traditional, but a half pound of ground beef browned with a teaspoon of the spice mix and spooned over one side of the platter works well for a mixed table.

Method

  1. Cook the onions first, and give them the time they need: 5 tablespoons olive oil in a wide skillet over medium, the sliced onions, and 25-30 minutes of occasional stirring until they are mahogany and sweet. Take a third of them out at the crisp stage for the top.
  2. Simmer the lentils in 4 cups of water for 15 minutes — cooked but still holding their shape. Drain, saving 2 cups of the cooking liquid.
  3. In the pot, combine lentils, drained rice, the two-thirds of the onions still in the pan with their oil, the cumin, coriander, cinnamon, salt and the 2 cups of reserved liquid.
  4. Bring to a boil, drop to the lowest heat, cover, and cook 18 minutes. Off the heat, lay a clean towel under the lid and let it stand 10 minutes — the towel catches the steam that would otherwise fall back and make it wet.
  5. Whisk the yogurt with the grated garlic, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.
  6. Fluff with a fork. Pile the reserved crisp onions on top, scatter parsley and sumac, and serve the garlic yogurt alongside.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Half an hour of onions is the recipe. There is no shortcut, and anybody who tells you otherwise has not eaten good mujaddara.
🧒 For the kids
Rice, lentils and sweet onions with yogurt — this is one of the safest bets on the whole list for a cautious eater.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 5 days, excellent cold, and improves. Traditionally eaten at room temperature.
