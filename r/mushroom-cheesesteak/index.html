Philly Mushroom Cheesesteak

Mushrooms and onions chopped on the griddle the way South Philly does it, piled into a roll with provolone melted over the top. The chopping is the technique.

F
25 min active 30 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 6 cups Cremini mushrooms, sliced thick
  • 2 Portobello mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 Yellow onion, sliced thin
  • 1 Green bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • 1 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 6 oz Part-skim mozzarella, sliced, or provolone
  • 4 Hoagie rolls

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Shaved ribeye

Half a pound of very thinly sliced steak, chopped on the griddle exactly the same way, added at the end so it cooks in 2 minutes.

Method

  1. Get the skillet properly hot. Add the oil, then the mushrooms in one layer, and leave them alone 4 minutes so they brown instead of stew.
  2. Add the onions and pepper and cook 6 minutes more.
  3. Now chop: work two spatulas or a bench scraper through the pile on the heat, cutting everything smaller as it cooks. Ten passes. This is what a Philly griddle cook does to a steak and it is why the filling packs into a roll instead of sliding out.
  4. Add the butter, soy sauce, salt and pepper. Cook 2 more minutes until nothing is watery. (A teaspoon of Worcestershire belongs here if nobody at the table is vegetarian — it contains anchovy.)
  5. Divide the pile into four roll-shaped heaps in the pan, lay cheese over each, and cover the skillet for 60 seconds to melt.
  6. Slide a split roll upside down over each heap, then scoop underneath with the spatula and flip the whole thing into the roll. That is the move.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The chop on the heat. It is one minute of work and it is what makes this taste like a cheesesteak rather than a mushroom sandwich.
🧒 For the kids
Cut the sandwich in half crosswise. It is mushrooms, onions and melted cheese in bread — no negotiation needed.
🥡 Leftovers
The filling keeps 3 days and is very good on eggs or pasta. Assemble to order.
