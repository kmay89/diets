One-Pot Creamy Lemon Orzo

The orzo cooks in the broth and thickens it with its own starch — creamy with no cream at all. Twenty-five minutes, one pot, dinner.

C
20 min active 25 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups Orzo (dry)
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Shallot, minced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 3 ½ cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 can Cannellini beans, canned no-salt-added, drained
  • 5 cups Baby spinach
  • 1 cup Frozen peas
  • 1 Lemon
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 3 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Shrimp or chicken

Shrimp poached in the broth for the last 3 minutes; or shredded rotisserie chicken stirred in at the end.

Method

  1. Cook the shallots in the oil over medium for 3 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the orzo and toast it, stirring, for 2 minutes until it smells nutty and looks slightly golden. This keeps the grains distinct instead of gluey.
  3. Add the broth and salt, bring to a simmer, and cook 9-10 minutes, stirring often, until the orzo is al dente and the liquid has gone creamy.
  4. Stir in the beans, peas and spinach and cook 2 minutes until the spinach collapses.
  5. Off the heat: the zest and juice of the lemon, the parmesan, dill and black pepper. It should be loose — it tightens fast, so serve it a little wetter than looks right.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Zest as well as juice. The zest carries the perfume; the juice only carries the sour.
🧒 For the kids
Small pasta, cheese and lemon. Leave the dill on the side if herbs are a problem.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 3 days and thickens solid — loosen with broth over low heat.
