Summer Corn and Zucchini over Creamy Polenta

August on a plate. Sweet corn and blistered zucchini over soft polenta, with goat cheese melting into it.

F
25 min active 40 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Cornmeal / polenta
  • 3 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 cup 1% milk
  • 3 tbsp Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Zucchini, half-moons
  • 3 ears Corn kernels, kernels cut off
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 cloves Garlic, sliced
  • ¼ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 oz Goat cheese (chèvre), crumbled
  • ⅜ cup Fresh basil, torn
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp juice Lemon

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add seared shrimp or sliced grilled chicken

Shrimp cook in the same skillet in 3 minutes after the vegetables come out.

Method

  1. Bring broth and milk to a bare simmer with half the salt. Rain in the cornmeal while whisking constantly — lumps form in the first 30 seconds or not at all.
  2. Cook on low 20-25 minutes, whisking every few minutes, until it is thick and no longer gritty. Off heat, stir in parmesan and butter.
  3. Meanwhile heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high with the olive oil. Add zucchini cut-side down in one layer and leave it alone 4 minutes until it blisters.
  4. Add corn, garlic and pepper flakes; 3 minutes. Add cherry tomatoes and cook 2 minutes just until they slump. Season, and finish with lemon juice.
  5. Spoon polenta into wide bowls, pile the vegetables on top, and scatter goat cheese and basil so it melts into the heat.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Wide shallow bowls, polenta spread with the back of a spoon rather than mounded, vegetables piled slightly off-center.
🧒 For the kids
Polenta is essentially savory porridge and most kids love it. Serve theirs with corn and cheese only, vegetables alongside.
🌿 From the garden
This exists to solve the August zucchini problem. Local Ohio sweet corn from a roadside stand is worth the detour.
🥡 Leftovers
Polenta sets solid in the fridge. Slice it into slabs and pan-fry in olive oil until crisp — a completely different, better dish.
