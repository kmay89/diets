Mediterranean Orzo Salad

Makes a bowl the size of a punchbowl, travels in a cooler, and is better an hour after you make it. The default potluck contribution.

20 min active 30 min total serves 12 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Orzo (dry)
  • 1 can Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 2 Cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 Red onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup Kalamata olives, halved
  • 1 jar Roasted red peppers (jarred), drained and chopped
  • 6 oz Feta cheese, crumbled
  • 6 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 5 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Red wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Dried oregano
  • 1 cup Flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 3 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp Black pepper

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add grilled chicken or shrimp

Sliced cold over the top, or folded through half the bowl.

Method

  1. Cook the orzo in well-salted water to just al dente, drain, and rinse under cold water until cool. Rinsing is right here even though it is wrong for hot pasta — it stops the cooking and removes the starch that would glue the salad together.
  2. Toss the warm-but-not-hot orzo with 2 tbsp of the oil so it stays loose.
  3. Whisk the remaining oil, lemon, vinegar, Dijon, oregano and pepper.
  4. Fold everything together except the feta and herbs. Let it sit 20 minutes — the orzo drinks the dressing, which is why this needs more dressing than you think.
  5. Fold in feta, parsley and dill just before serving. Taste and add lemon; it almost always wants more.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Herbs at the end, in quantity. A cup of parsley is not a garnish, it is an ingredient.
🧒 For the kids
Fill a small container with plain buttered orzo, cucumber and feta before you dress the big bowl.
🌿 From the garden
Cucumbers, tomatoes, parsley and dill all at once in July.
🥡 Leftovers
4 days. Add a squeeze of lemon before serving again — it flattens overnight.
