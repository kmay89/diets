Whipped Feta with Hot Honey and Pistachios

Feta and yogurt beaten until it turns into something closer to a cloud, then flooded with warm chile honey. It takes eight minutes and it is always the first bowl to empty.

10 min active 10 min total serves 8 vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Feta cheese, crumbled
  • ½ cup Plain 2% Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1 tsp zest Lemon
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 3 tbsp Honey
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • ⅜ cup Pistachios, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint, torn
  • 6 Whole wheat pita, cut in wedges and toasted

Method

  1. Put the feta, yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic clove and pepper in a food processor and run it for a full 2 minutes, scraping down twice.
  2. Two minutes is longer than feels necessary and it is the whole recipe. Under-processed it is a dip; properly whipped it is aerated and holds a swoosh.
  3. Warm the honey with the pepper flakes in a small pan or 20 seconds in the microwave, just enough to loosen it and let the chile bloom.
  4. Spread the whipped feta across a shallow plate with the back of a spoon, leaving ridges. Ridges catch honey; a flat surface lets it run to the edge.
  5. Pour the hot honey over the middle, scatter the pistachios and mint, and serve with warm pita.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Chill the plate first. The honey sets slightly where it hits the cold feta and stays in a pool instead of sliding off.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the pepper flakes out of half the honey. Plain honey over whipped feta with pita is a genuinely good kid snack — it reads as cream cheese.
🌿 From the garden
Mint is unstoppable here — grow it in a sunken pot so it cannot run. One plant covers a summer of this.
🥡 Leftovers
The whipped feta keeps 5 days and firms up in the fridge; let it sit out 20 minutes and stir before serving. Add honey fresh each time.
