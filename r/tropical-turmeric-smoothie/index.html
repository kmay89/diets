Pineapple Turmeric Ginger Smoothie

Pineapple, mango, fresh ginger and turmeric with a crack of black pepper — the pepper is there for a reason. Bright, tart and genuinely refreshing.

5 min active 5 min total serves 2 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups Frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 cup Frozen mango chunks
  • 1 ¼ cups Oat milk
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • ⅛ tsp Black pepper
  • 1 Lime
  • 2 tbsp Hemp hearts

Method

  1. Blend everything until smooth.
  2. The black pepper is not a garnish: piperine substantially increases how much curcumin the body absorbs, which is why turmeric and pepper appear together in almost every cuisine that uses turmeric.
  3. Serve very cold. Turmeric stains — rinse the blender straight away.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Fresh ginger, grated on a microplane. Ground ginger tastes like a different plant.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the pepper out and cut the ginger in half. It is pineapple and mango underneath.
🥡 Leftovers
Drink fresh.
