White Bean Melt

Twelve minutes, mostly under the broiler. Crunchy, savory, melty, and built entirely from things already in the cupboard.

D
12 min active 12 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 can Cannellini beans, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 3 tbsp Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 stalk Celery, finely diced
  • ¼ Red onion, minced
  • 1 tbsp Capers, rinsed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • 4 slices Whole grain bread
  • 3 oz Sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 Roma tomatoes, sliced
  • ½ tsp Black pepper

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Make it a tuna melt

A drained can of tuna folded into half the mixture is the classic.

Method

  1. Mash the beans roughly with the yogurt, Dijon, lemon and pepper — leave some whole for texture.
  2. Fold in celery, red onion, capers and dill.
  3. Toast the bread on a sheet pan under the broiler for 90 seconds a side.
  4. Pile the bean mixture on, top with tomato and cheese, and broil 2-3 minutes until the cheese blisters. Watch it — broilers go from perfect to charcoal in about twenty seconds.
  5. Eat immediately with a knife and fork.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Rinsed capers. A tablespoon adds the briny pop people cannot identify but always notice.
🧒 For the kids
Skip the capers and onion, cut into fingers.
🌿 From the garden
Dill and tomatoes.
🥡 Leftovers
The bean salad keeps 3 days; assemble to order.
