Roasted Salsa Verde

Tomatillos and chiles blackened under the broiler, then blended with raw garlic and cilantro. Fifteen minutes for a jar that improves everything it touches.

10 min active 25 min total serves 10 vegetarianvegan

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lb Tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 2 Jalapeño
  • 1 Serrano pepper
  • ½ Yellow onion, in thick wedges
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 cup Cilantro
  • 1 Lime
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Broil the tomatillos, chiles and onion on a foil-lined pan until blistered and blackened in patches, 8-10 minutes, turning once. The tomatillos will collapse and leak — keep the juice.
  2. Blend the roasted vegetables with the raw garlic, cilantro, lime and salt. Raw garlic in a cooked salsa gives it a sharpness the roasted kind loses.
  3. Pulse rather than purée: a salsa should have texture.
  4. Taste for salt and acid. Tomatillos vary a lot in sourness, so this is not optional.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Foil under the broiler and every drop of tomatillo juice scraped back into the blender. That juice is the body of the sauce.
🧒 For the kids
Use one jalapeño with seeds removed. Roasted tomatillo is tangy and mild on its own.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 10 days and freezes 3 months. Good on eggs, tacos, beans, roast vegetables and, honestly, most things.
