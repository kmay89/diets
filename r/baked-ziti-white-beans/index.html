Big-Batch Baked Ziti with White Beans

Feeds twelve, travels in the dish you baked it in, and reheats without complaint. The potluck workhorse.

C
25 min active 70 min total serves 12 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Whole wheat pasta (dry), ziti or penne
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, diced
  • 5 cloves Garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 can Crushed tomatoes, no salt added
  • 2 cans Cannellini beans, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 5 cups Baby spinach
  • 15 oz Part-skim ricotta
  • 8 oz Part-skim mozzarella, shredded
  • 6 tbsp Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 Eggs (large)
  • ½ cup Fresh basil, torn
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add browned sausage or turkey

Brown separately, and stir into half the sauce if you need both versions in one dish.

Method

  1. Boil the pasta 2 minutes short of al dente — it finishes in the oven, and fully cooked pasta here turns to mush.
  2. Cook the onion in oil 7 minutes; add garlic, oregano and pepper flakes for a minute; fry the tomato paste 90 seconds until it darkens.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes, one can of beans and the salt. Simmer 10 minutes. Blend the second can of beans with 1/2 cup water until smooth and stir it in — that is the creaminess, without cream.
  4. Wilt the spinach into the sauce. Mix the ricotta with the egg, half the parmesan and plenty of pepper.
  5. Layer in a 9x13: sauce, pasta, dollops of ricotta, mozzarella. Repeat. Finish with the rest of the parmesan.
  6. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes covered, 15 uncovered until bubbling and browned. Rest 15 minutes before cutting, and scatter basil over.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Rest it a full 15 minutes. Baked pasta cut straight from the oven slumps onto the plate; rested, it holds a square.
🧒 For the kids
This is the meal children request. If yours object to spinach, blend it into the sauce with the beans and it vanishes.
🌿 From the garden
Basil, and tomatoes if you cook down your own sauce in September.
🥡 Leftovers
5 days. Freezes 3 months baked or unbaked.
