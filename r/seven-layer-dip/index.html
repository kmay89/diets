Seven-Layer Bean Dip

The game-day dish that people stand around. Built on real refried beans and Greek yogurt instead of sour cream, and nobody can tell.

20 min active 20 min total serves 12 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Pinto beans, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 Chipotles in adobo, minced
  • 2 Lime
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 Avocado
  • 1 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup Salsa, drained
  • 4 oz Sharp cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, diced
  • 4 Scallions (green onions), sliced
  • ⅜ cup Kalamata olives, sliced
  • ½ cup Cilantro, chopped
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Blend the pinto beans with cumin, smoked paprika, chipotle, the juice of one lime, the oil and 2 tbsp water until spreadable. Spread in the bottom of a glass dish so the layers show.
  2. Mash the avocados with the second lime and salt; spread over the beans, right to the edges so they do not brown.
  3. Spread the yogurt, then the drained salsa. Draining matters — undrained salsa turns the whole thing into a puddle by halftime.
  4. Cheese, tomatoes, scallions, olives, cilantro.
  5. Refrigerate up to 4 hours. Serve with sturdy chips or cut vegetables.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Use a straight-sided glass dish. Half the appeal of this thing is being able to see the layers.
🧒 For the kids
Skip the chipotle in the beans and keep one corner plain beans-cheese-chips.
🌿 From the garden
Tomatoes, scallions and cilantro.
🥡 Leftovers
2 days, though the avocado layer dulls. Best made the day of.
