Miso Noodle Bowl with Bok Choy

A ten-minute broth that tastes like it simmered with bones. Miso goes in off the heat, which is the rule that makes or breaks it.

F
20 min active 30 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 6 cups Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Fresh ginger, sliced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, smashed
  • 4 oz Shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced
  • 3 tbsp White miso paste
  • 1 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 block Extra-firm tofu, cubed
  • 4 Baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 cup Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 8 oz Rice noodles (dry)
  • 4 Scallions (green onions), sliced
  • 2 tsp Toasted sesame oil
  • 4 Eggs (large), soft-boiled, halved (optional)
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add shredded chicken

Poach a breast right in the broth at the start, then shred it.

Method

  1. Simmer broth with ginger and garlic for 10 minutes, then fish them out. Add shiitakes and simmer 5 more.
  2. Cook the rice noodles separately per the package and rinse them — cooking them in the broth turns it cloudy and gluey.
  3. Add tofu, bok choy and edamame to the broth; simmer 3 minutes until the greens are just tender.
  4. Off the heat — genuinely off, not low — whisk the miso with a ladle of hot broth in a small bowl until smooth, then stir it back in. Boiling miso kills the flavor and the live cultures.
  5. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Taste; it should be savory and round.
  6. Divide noodles into bowls, ladle broth and vegetables over, and top with scallions, pepper flakes and a soft-boiled egg half.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
The soft-boiled egg: 6 minutes 30 seconds in boiling water, then ice water. Jammy yolk, every time.
🧒 For the kids
Serve deconstructed: noodles in the bowl, a little broth, edamame on the side. Most kids negotiate over the broth, not the noodles.
🌿 From the garden
Bok choy grows in 45 days and tolerates the cold snaps that kill everything else in April.
🥡 Leftovers
Store noodles and broth separately or you will find soup with no noodles and noodles with no soup.
