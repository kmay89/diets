Fifteen-Minute Chickpea Skillet

Pantry to plate in the time it takes to set the table. No chopping board required if you buy the spinach pre-washed.

A
15 min active 15 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 cloves Garlic, sliced
  • ½ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 2 cans Chickpeas, canned no-salt-added, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can Diced tomatoes, no salt added
  • 5 cups Baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • ½ cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt (optional)
  • 4 slices Whole grain bread, toasted
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Start the garlic in cold oil over medium with the pepper flakes. By the time it sizzles and turns pale gold, about 3 minutes, the flavour is in the oil.
  2. Add cumin, smoked paprika and tomato paste; fry 60 seconds until it darkens.
  3. Add chickpeas and tomatoes with the salt. Simmer hard for 6 minutes, smashing about a third of the chickpeas against the pan so the sauce thickens.
  4. Stir in the spinach until it wilts, 1 minute. Finish with lemon juice.
  5. Spoon over toast with a dollop of yogurt. Dinner, from a cupboard, in a quarter of an hour.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Smashing a third of the chickpeas. It takes ten seconds and turns a loose pan of beans into a sauce that clings.
🧒 For the kids
Skip the pepper flakes and serve over rice or pasta instead of toast.
🌿 From the garden
Spinach in spring and autumn.
🥡 Leftovers
4 days, and it thickens into something you can put in a wrap.
