Skillet Paneer Tikka Masala

Cubes of paneer browned in butter and folded into a tomato sauce built on bloomed garam masala. Restaurant tikka masala usually runs on a cup of cream; this one uses three tablespoons and gets the rest of its body from blended onion.

D
25 min active 40 min total serves 5 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 package Paneer, cut in 3/4-inch cubes
  • 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp Canola oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, roughly chopped
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 can Crushed tomatoes, no salt added
  • ¾ cup Low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp Heavy cream
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 cup Frozen peas
  • ½ cup Cilantro, chopped
  • 1 ½ cups Brown rice (dry), uncooked
  • 1 tbsp juice Lemon

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Add chicken thigh

Brown it in the same pan before the paneer and add it back with the peas. The sauce was designed to take either.

Method

  1. Start the rice. Pat the paneer cubes dry — wet paneer will not brown, it will squeak.
  2. Melt 1 tablespoon of the butter with the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Brown the paneer on two or three sides, about 5 minutes total, and lift it out. It should be golden and slightly crisp; it will soften again in the sauce.
  3. Add the onion to the same pan and cook 8 minutes until soft and browning. Add the garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute.
  4. Add the remaining butter, then all the ground spices, and stir constantly for 60 seconds. Blooming them in fat rather than dumping them into liquid is the single biggest difference between this and a flat curry.
  5. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 minutes until it darkens. Add the crushed tomatoes and broth, scrape the bottom, and simmer 12 minutes.
  6. Blend the sauce smooth — an immersion blender in the pan is easiest. This is what gives it the silky body that would otherwise take a cup of cream.
  7. Stir in the cream, salt and peas, return the paneer, and simmer 4 minutes more. Finish with lemon juice off the heat and a heap of cilantro. Serve over the rice.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Swirl the last spoonful of cream over the top in a spiral rather than stirring it in, and drop the cilantro on at the very last second. Both things happen at the pass in a restaurant for exactly this reason.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the pepper flakes out and this is mild — garam masala is aromatic, not hot. Paneer holds its shape and does not string, which makes it an easier cheese for a suspicious kid than melted mozzarella.
🌿 From the garden
Cilantro and hot peppers both do well here. Ginger will grow in a pot on a sunny porch through an Ohio summer and gives you tender young rhizomes in October.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and freezes 2 months, though the paneer firms up after freezing. Reheat gently — a hard boil makes the cream break.
