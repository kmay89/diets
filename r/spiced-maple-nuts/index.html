Rosemary Maple Spiced Nuts

Twenty minutes, one pan, and a jar of the thing everyone circles at a party. Also the best homemade gift in December.

10 min active 30 min total serves 10 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Raw almonds
  • 1 cup Pecans
  • 1 cup Walnuts
  • 3 tbsp Pure maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh rosemary, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 Eggs (large), white only, beaten to a froth

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Low and slow — nuts scorch on the inside long before they look dark.
  2. Whisk the egg white to a loose froth and toss the nuts in it. This is what makes the coating cling and dry into a crackly shell instead of sliding off.
  3. Add the maple, oil, rosemary, paprika, cayenne and salt and toss until every nut is coated.
  4. Spread on a parchment-lined pan and bake 22-25 minutes, stirring twice.
  5. Cool completely on the pan without touching them. They are soft out of the oven and crisp up as they cool — this is where people go wrong and decide the recipe failed.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Fresh rosemary, chopped fine, added before baking so it toasts into the coating.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the cayenne out and add a pinch of cinnamon for a sweeter version.
🥡 Leftovers
Two weeks in a sealed jar at room temperature. Do not refrigerate — they go soft.
