Jackfruit Tacos al Pastor

Chile-marinated, charred at the edges, sweet with pineapple — the trompo flavor in a skillet, with jackfruit doing an uncannily good impression of the pork.

D
25 min active 40 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cans Young green jackfruit (canned), drained, rinsed, shredded
  • 3 Dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 Chipotles in adobo
  • 2 cups Pineapple, cut into small chunks
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp Ground allspice
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • 12 Corn tortillas
  • ½ Red onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup Cilantro, chopped
  • 2 Lime, wedges

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

The original, with pork

Thin-sliced pork shoulder in the same marinade for at least 4 hours, then seared hard in a very hot pan. It is one of the great marinades in the world.

Method

  1. Soak the guajillos in boiling water 15 minutes. Blend them with the chipotles, half a cup of the pineapple, garlic, vinegar, cumin, oregano, allspice and salt into a marinade.
  2. Squeeze the shredded jackfruit dry in a towel — canned jackfruit is packed wet and will steam otherwise. Toss it with the marinade and leave it 15 minutes, or overnight.
  3. Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high. Spread the jackfruit in one layer and leave it alone 4 minutes, then turn and repeat, twice more, until the edges are dark and crisp. Those crisp edges are exactly what a real trompo gives you.
  4. Push it to one side, add the rest of the pineapple to the bare metal, and let it char 3 minutes.
  5. Warm the tortillas directly over a flame or in a dry skillet until they puff and spot.
  6. Build with two tortillas each, jackfruit, charred pineapple, onion, cilantro and a hard squeeze of lime.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Two small corn tortillas per taco, warmed over a live flame. It is what keeps the taco from falling apart and it takes ten seconds each.
🧒 For the kids
Sweet, mildly spicy and shreddable. Serve theirs without the raw onion and with extra pineapple.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and crisps up again beautifully in a hot pan.
