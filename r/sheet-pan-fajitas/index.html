Sheet-Pan Fajitas

One tray, 425°F, and the char comes from the pan being preheated. Everything is on the table in half an hour and there is one thing to wash.

A
15 min active 40 min total serves 5 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 Red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 Green bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 Yellow onion, sliced thick
  • 3 Portobello mushrooms, sliced thick
  • 1 can Black beans, canned no-salt-added, drained
  • 3 tbsp Avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp Chili powder
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt
  • 2 Lime
  • 12 Corn tortillas
  • 1 Avocado, sliced
  • ½ cup Cilantro
  • ½ cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Chicken or steak on the same pan

Sliced thigh or skirt steak in the same spice mix, on its own half of a second pan so the juices do not steam the vegetables.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F with the sheet pan inside for 10 minutes. Vegetables hitting hot metal sear; vegetables hitting cold metal sweat.
  2. Toss the peppers, onions and mushrooms with the oil, all the spices and the salt.
  3. Spread them on the hot pan in one layer — use two pans rather than crowding one — and roast 20 minutes.
  4. Add the black beans, toss, and roast 5 minutes more until the edges are charred in places.
  5. Squeeze a whole lime over the hot pan and scrape everything together with the juices.
  6. Warm the tortillas. Serve with avocado, cilantro, yogurt and the second lime.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Preheat the pan. It is the difference between roasted vegetables and fajitas.
🧒 For the kids
Build-your-own is most of the appeal. Put everything in bowls in the middle of the table.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and makes excellent quesadillas, burritos and grain bowls.
