Ginataang Gulay

Squash and long beans stewed in coconut milk with ginger and garlic. Filipino home cooking at its plainest and best, and it is done in half an hour.

D
15 min active 35 min total serves 4 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 4 cups Butternut squash, cubed
  • 3 cups Green beans, cut into 2-inch lengths
  • 2 cans Light coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp Canola oil
  • 1 Yellow onion, sliced
  • 6 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Fresh ginger, julienned
  • 1 block Extra-firm tofu, cubed and browned
  • 2 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 Thai bird chiles, split (optional)
  • 4 cups Baby spinach
  • ½ tsp Kosher salt
  • 1 ½ cups Jasmine rice (dry)

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Shrimp or pork

Shrimp in the last 3 minutes, or thin-sliced pork browned at the start with the aromatics.

Method

  1. Start the rice. Brown the tofu cubes in the oil and set them aside.
  2. Cook the onion, garlic and ginger in the same pot for 4 minutes. Filipino cooks brown the garlic more than most — take it to properly gold.
  3. Add the coconut milk, soy sauce and chiles and bring to a gentle simmer. Never a hard boil, or the coconut milk separates into oil and curd.
  4. Add the squash and simmer 12 minutes, then the green beans for 6 more, until the squash is tender enough to crush against the side of the pot.
  5. Return the tofu, stir in the spinach to wilt, and taste for salt. Serve over rice with the sauce spooned generously over.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Julienne the ginger rather than mincing it. You want to find pieces of it.
🧒 For the kids
Leave the chiles whole and lift them out. Sweet squash in coconut milk over rice is a mild, friendly plate.
🥡 Leftovers
Keeps 4 days and thickens. Loosen with a splash of water.
