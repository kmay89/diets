Cherry Cocoa Smoothie

Frozen dark cherries, real cocoa and a spoonful of almond butter. Tastes like a milkshake, and tart cherries have a small but genuine literature behind them for sleep and soreness.

5 min active 5 min total serves 2 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Frozen dark sweet cherries
  • 2 tbsp Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp Almond butter
  • 1 Bananas
  • 1 ¼ cups Unsweetened soy milk
  • 1 tsp Pure maple syrup
  • ½ tsp Vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp Kosher salt

Method

  1. Blend everything 60-90 seconds until there is no grit from the cocoa left.
  2. Cocoa needs fat and a little sweetness to stop tasting chalky — the almond butter and maple are both doing structural work here.
  3. Taste and add a splash more milk if it is too thick to drink through a straw.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
A pinch of salt and half a teaspoon of vanilla. Both make cocoa taste more like chocolate.
🧒 For the kids
This is a chocolate milkshake as far as most children are concerned.
🥡 Leftovers
Drink fresh.
