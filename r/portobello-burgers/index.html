Marinated Portobello Burgers

The mushroom burger that is not an apology: marinated in balsamic and smoke, grilled until it gives up its juice, stacked with charred onion and garlic aioli.

F
20 min active 50 min total serves 4 vegetarian kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 4 Portobello mushrooms, stems and gills removed
  • 3 tbsp Balsamic vinegar
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 4 cloves Garlic, three minced, one grated
  • 1 Sweet onion, cut into thick rings
  • 4 tbsp Olive oil mayonnaise
  • ½ Lemon
  • 4 Whole wheat burger buns
  • 3 oz Part-skim mozzarella, sliced (optional)
  • 4 leaf Butter lettuce
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, sliced

🍽 Fork in the road — for the omnivores

Beef patties alongside

Same grill, same buns, same aioli. Season the patties only with salt and pepper and do not press them.

Method

  1. Whisk the balsamic, 2 tablespoons oil, soy sauce, paprika and minced garlic. Marinate the mushrooms gill side up for 30 minutes so the caps fill like bowls.
  2. Scrape the gills out before marinating — they hold a lot of water and they muddy everything they touch.
  3. Mix the aioli: mayonnaise, grated garlic, lemon juice.
  4. Grill the mushrooms gill side up first, 5 minutes, so the juices pool. Flip and grill 4 more, and put the cheese on now if you are using it. Brush the onion rings with the last oil and grill them alongside.
  5. Toast the buns cut side down for 30 seconds.
  6. Build: aioli on both halves, lettuce, mushroom, onion, tomato. Eat it before the juices soak the bun.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Gill side up first. That five minutes turns the cap into a bowl of concentrated mushroom juice instead of a puddle on the grate.
🧒 For the kids
Cut it in half and add the cheese. A grilled mushroom cap in a bun with melted mozzarella is a genuinely easy sell.
🥡 Leftovers
Sliced cold portobello is excellent in a grain bowl the next day. Keeps 3 days.
