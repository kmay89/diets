Any-Vegetable Roast (the Method)

One temperature, one technique, every vegetable. This is the skill that makes vegetables the best thing on the plate instead of the obligation.

10 min active 35 min total serves 6 vegetarianvegan kids eat it

Ingredients

  • 1 head Broccoli, florets — or substitute any vegetable below
  • 4 Carrots, cut on the bias
  • 2 cups Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 3 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¾ tsp Kosher salt
  • ½ tsp Black pepper
  • 1 Lemon, cut into wedges
  • 3 cloves Garlic, smashed (optional)

Method

  1. 425°F, always. Lower and vegetables steam; higher and the outside burns before the inside softens.
  2. Cut everything the same size, then toss with enough oil that every surface is glossy — under-oiling is the most common reason home roasted vegetables are leathery instead of caramelized.
  3. Spread on the pan with actual space between pieces. Two pans is not a failure, it is the recipe. Crowding is the second most common reason.
  4. Put the cut sides down and do not stir for the first 15 minutes. Contact with hot metal is where browning happens.
  5. TIMING: broccoli, cauliflower, asparagus, green beans 20-25 min. Brussels sprouts, carrots, sweet potato, squash 25-35 min. Zucchini, peppers, onions 20 min. Tomatoes 25 min.
  6. Finish with acid off the heat — lemon, a splash of vinegar, or balsamic. That final squeeze is what makes people go back for more.

Notes

✨ The restaurant touch
Preheat the sheet pan in the oven and add the vegetables to hot metal. Immediate sizzle, better crust.
🧒 For the kids
Roast a batch of 'broccoli trees' or 'carrot coins' with nothing but oil and salt. Kids often reject seasoning, not vegetables.
🌿 From the garden
This is the method that absorbs anything the garden produces in quantity.
🥡 Leftovers
4 days. Cold roasted vegetables belong in tomorrow's grain bowl, eggs, or a sandwich.
